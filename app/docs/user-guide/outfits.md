# User Guide - Generating Outfits

Creating outfit recommendations and rating combinations.

## About Outfits

An **outfit** is a combination of clothing items recommended by the AI. The system:
- 🤖 Uses neural networks to analyze compatibility
- 📊 Learns from your ratings
- ⭐ Suggests personalized combinations
- 📈 Improves over time

### Why Use This Feature

✅ **Save Time**: Get ready faster  
✅ **Discover Combinations**: Find new pairings  
✅ **Consistent Style**: Recommended items work together  
✅ **Personalized**: Learns your preferences  
✅ **No Decision Fatigue**: Suggestions ready to go  

---

## Generating Outfits

### Before You Start

**Requirements:**
- ✅ Wardrobe with at least 3 items
- ✅ Items from different categories (tops, bottoms, shoes)
- ✅ Good quality item photos
- ✅ Accurate item metadata

### Generating Outfit (Basic)

**Step 1: Open Wardrobe**
1. Click "Wardrobe" in navigation
2. Select desired wardrobe
3. Ensure it has items

**Step 2: Generate Outfit**
1. Click "Generate Outfit" button
2. Or click "Outfit Generator" tab
3. System analyzes items
4. Outfit appears in 2-10 seconds

**Step 3: View Result**
Shows:
- 📸 Item photos arranged
- 👕 Clothing pieces selected
- ⭐ Compatibility score (0-5 stars)
- ✔️/❌ Like or dislike buttons

### Understanding Score

**Rating System:**

| Stars | Meaning | Interpretation |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Perfect fit | Definitely wear this |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Excellent | Great combination |
| ⭐⭐⭐ | Good | Will look nice |
| ⭐⭐ | Fair | Not ideal |
| ⭐ | Poor | Not recommended |

**How Score is Calculated:**
1. Neural network analyzes items
2. Considers color harmony
3. Checks category compatibility
4. Uses your preference history
5. Generates 0-5 star rating

### Generating Multiple Outfits

**Getting Options:**
1. Generate outfit
2. Dislike it? Click "Get Another"
3. Generates new combination
4. Keep clicking until satisfied

**Viewing Favorites:**
1. Generate outfit you like
2. Click heart/favorite icon
3. Outfit saved for later
4. Access in "Saved Outfits"

## Customizing Generation

### Basic Options

**Number of Items:**
- Default: 3-4 items
- Options: 2-5 items
- More items = longer outfit

**Occasion Type:**
- **Casual**: Everyday wear (jeans, t-shirt, sneakers)
- **Work**: Professional (slacks, blouse, heels)
- **Formal**: Special events (dress, heels, accessories)
- **Sports**: Athletic (activewear, sneakers)
- **Evening**: Night out (dress, heels, accessories)

**Selecting Options:**
1. Click "Generation Settings" or gear icon ⚙️
2. Choose occasion
3. Select number of items
4. Click "Generate"

### Advanced Options

**Exclude Items:**
- Click item in wardrobe
- Select "Exclude from generation"
- Won't appear in future outfits
- Until re-enabled

**Focus on Category:**
- Must include specific type
- Example: "Must include blazer"
- System prioritizes that category

**Color Preferences:**
- Avoid certain colors
- Prefer specific colors
- "Use lots of blue"
- Or "No red items"

## Rating Outfits

### Why Rate Outfits?

**Rating Benefits:**
✅ Teaches system your preferences  
✅ Improves future recommendations  
✅ Triggers AI model training  
✅ Personalizes results  
✅ Only takes 5 seconds  

### How to Rate

**Immediate Rating (After Generation):**
1. View generated outfit
2. Decide if you like it
3. Click ⭐ star rating (1-5)
4. Or click 👍/👎 buttons
5. Optional: Add notes/comments
6. Rating saved

**Rating Scale:**

| Rating | What It Means | When to Use |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5) | Love it! | Would definitely wear |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4) | Great outfit | Great combination |
| ⭐⭐⭐ (3) | Okay | Would wear if needed |
| ⭐⭐ (2) | Don't like | Wouldn't choose this |
| ⭐ (1) | Bad combo | Would never wear |

### Adding Notes

**Comment on Outfit:**
1. After rating
2. Click "Add Note"
3. Write comment:
   - "Better with different shoes"
   - "Love blue with black"
   - "Good for work"
   - "Not my style"
4. Save

**Why Comments Help:**
- System learns specific preferences
- Improves future recommendations
- Personal reference
- Feedback for you later

### Rating History

**View Past Outfits:**
1. Click "Outfit History" tab
2. See all generated outfits
3. Filter by rating
4. Show rated/unrated only

**Update Ratings:**
1. Click outfit in history
2. Change rating
3. Update notes
4. System re-learns

## AI Training System

### Auto-Training

**How It Works:**
1. You rate outfits
2. After 10 ratings:
   - System creates personal model
   - Trains on your preferences
   - Saves improved model
   - Counter resets to 0
3. Next generation uses improved model
4. Better recommendations!

### Training Timeline

```
Outfit 1: Rate it → Total: 1
Outfit 2: Rate it → Total: 2
...
Outfit 9: Rate it → Total: 9
Outfit 10: Rate it → TRAINING TRIGGERED!
├─ System trains model (5-10 seconds)
├─ Saves to your device
└─ Next outfit uses improved model

Outfit 11: Better recommendations!
(Counter resets to 0)
```

### Checking Training Status

1. Go to Settings
2. Click "Model Status" or "AI Training"
3. View:
   - Training count
   - Ratings processed
   - Model accuracy
   - Next training countdown

### Improving Model

**To get better recommendations:**
✅ Rate outfits consistently  
✅ Rate honestly, not randomly  
✅ Try different occasions  
✅ Rate variety of combinations  
✅ Give mixed ratings (not all 5s)  

**What To Avoid:**
❌ Rating randomly  
❌ Rating all outfits same  
❌ Not providing feedback  
❌ Ignoring generated outfits

## Troubleshooting

### Not Getting Good Suggestions

**Check:**
1. Wardrobe has 3+ items
2. Items diverse (different categories)
3. Item photos are quality
4. Item information accurate
5. Haven't rated many outfits yet

**Solutions:**
1. Upload more items
2. Add items from missing categories
3. Rate more outfits
4. Wait for model training
5. Improve item photos

### Same Outfit Generated Twice

**Normal Behavior:**
- Sometimes happens with small wardrobes
- Click "Get Another" to skip
- More items = more variety
- More ratings = better diversity

### System Says "Need More Items"

**Solutions:**
1. Wardrobe needs minimum 3 items
2. Try different categories together
3. Add items from missing categories
4. Example: Pants + Top + Shoes

### Model Training Slow

**Normal Timeline:**
- First training: 5-10 seconds
- Subsequent trainings: 3-5 seconds
- Happens in background
- You can use app while training
